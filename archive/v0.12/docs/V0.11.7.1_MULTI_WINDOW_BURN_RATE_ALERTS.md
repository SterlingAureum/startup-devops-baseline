# v0.11.7.1 Multi-window Burn-rate Alerts

## Outcome

This increment turns the accepted demo-api availability and latency SLOs into
actionable, low-noise budget-consumption signals. It adds no automatic delivery
decision: operators remain responsible for diagnosis and mitigation.

## Policy

| Class | Paired windows | Threshold | Severity |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Fast | 5m and 1h | 14.4 | critical |
| Sustained fast | 30m and 6h | 6 | critical |
| Slow | 2h and 1d | 3 | warning |
| Long slow | 6h and 3d | 1 | warning |

A fast alert fires when either critical pair breaches. A slow alert fires when
either warning pair breaches. Within every pair, both windows must breach.

Availability burn rate divides the observed `5xx` fraction by the allowed
0.1% bad fraction. Latency burn rate divides the fraction above 500 ms by the
allowed 1% bad fraction. A burn rate of 1 is exactly sustainable across the
30-day window. No eligible traffic produces no series; it is never converted
into a healthy zero.
