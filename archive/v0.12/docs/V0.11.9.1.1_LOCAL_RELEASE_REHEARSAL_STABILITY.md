# v0.11.9.1.1 Local rehearsal stability closure

This increment closes issues observed during the successful v0.11.9.1 local
rollout rehearsal without changing its release policy.

## Closed observations

- Phase subprocess output is mirrored live to the operator terminal while the
  identical stream remains in the attempt `command.log`.
- Post-command image verification tolerates the bounded transient period in
  which a newly scaled Canary Pod exists but is not Ready or lacks imageID.
- Assignment-form bundle input such as `REHEARSAL_DIR=/tmp/...` is rejected with
  a raw-path example instead of a misleading repository-evidence error.
- The active runbook now names terminals, visible handoff signals, exact promote
  timing, log discovery commands and a phase-specific retry decision table.

The convergence wait is bounded to 120 seconds and does not conceal an unhealthy
Pod. The phase command remains bounded to 900 seconds. Neither wait performs a
mutation. Promotion, retry and rollback remain human-owned and explicit.

The operator's successful v0.11.9.1 evidence is historical input to this repair;
offline tests for v0.11.9.1.1 do not claim another live execution. Controlled
failure and recovery remain the next v0.11.9.2 checkpoint.
