# v0.11.9.2.2.2.1.1 offline lifecycle environment isolation

The full quality gate inherited an operator `CLUSTER_NAME` from the local kind
session. The v0.9 lifecycle validator uses fake AWS, Terraform and kubectl
clients, but its aws-test context helper previously accepted that ambient name.
The fake destroy output therefore differed from the validator's fixed aws-test
assertion even though no real EKS environment existed or was contacted.

The validator now clears local-kind and cloud identity overrides before creating
its fixtures. Its individual test invocations continue to set the exact values
they own. The focused restoration guard also runs the lifecycle regression so
this isolation remains covered.

Applying and validating this increment performs no cluster, AWS, Terraform,
Rollout, traffic, retry or promotion operation.
