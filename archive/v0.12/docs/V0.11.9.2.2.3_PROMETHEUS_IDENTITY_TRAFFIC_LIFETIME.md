# v0.11.9.2.2.3 Prometheus identity and traffic lifetime

## Observation

The reviewed revision 67 retry created AnalysisRun `67-2.1`. Kubernetes Canary
identity checks passed and the observer sent 44 stable/Canary request pairs, but
the three Candidate request queries returned empty vectors. Target-up and stable
budget queries succeeded. After failure, Rollout abort moved the Canary Service
selector back to the stable ReplicaSet, so post-failure identity diagnostics
could not prove what Prometheus observed during the request window.

## Repair

Before sending traffic, the observer now port-forwards Prometheus and requires
an `up` series carrying both `job="demo-api-canary"` and the exact expected
`platform_release_id`. Traffic then runs in a background producer until the
selected new AnalysisRun reaches a terminal phase. The producer still enforces
the existing minimum request count and duration, but no longer stops before
delayed analysis measurements finish.

The AnalysisTemplate target-up metric is also scoped to the exact release ID.
This prevents a stable target temporarily selected by the Canary Service from
satisfying Candidate target readiness.

The observer never promotes, retries or aborts. Failed revisions 66 and 67
remain evidence and must not be retried again. After this repair is committed
and pushed, recovery must use a new application version and therefore a new
Rollout revision; the exact launch procedure is reviewed separately after the
current runtime and desired image identities are captured.

Applying and validating this increment performs no live Kubernetes or AWS
operation and generates no traffic.
