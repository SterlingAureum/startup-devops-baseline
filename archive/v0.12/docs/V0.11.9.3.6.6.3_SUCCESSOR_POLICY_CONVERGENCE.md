# v0.11.9.3.6.6.3 successor policy convergence

The held aws-dev to aws-test promotion exposed several inherited validators
that treated an old release snapshot as permanent current state. This repair
keeps their historical contracts unchanged but routes every remaining mutable
aws-test check in the active main-CI chain through one reviewed two-state
successor policy.

The allowed states remain exact file identities: the historical aws-test
release or the reviewed candidate. Unknown, partial, and mixed identities fail
closed. The aws-prod release remains byte-for-byte pinned until its own
separately reviewed promotion.

No workflow, PR update, merge, cloud access, deployment, or teardown is
performed or authorized by this increment.
